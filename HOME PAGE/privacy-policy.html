Privacy Policy

Go Agadir Travel respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you browse our website or send a booking request.

Information We Collect

When you request a booking, we may collect your name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number, selected activity, travel date, group size, pickup information, add-ons, notes, and other booking details you choose to provide.

How We Use Client Information

We use your information to respond to booking requests, confirm availability, coordinate tour details, provide customer support, send booking emails, and improve our travel services in Agadir.

Cookies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to keep the site working, remember basic preferences, measure performance, and support advertising features. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Google AdSense and Third-Party Ads

We use Google AdSense and may display third-party ads. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on visits to this and other websites. You can manage ad personalization through your Google ad settings.

Google Analytics

If Google Analytics or similar analytics tools are used on this website, they help us understand general visitor behavior, page performance, and traffic sources. Analytics data is used in aggregate to improve the website.

Resend Email Service

Booking request emails may be processed through Resend, our email service provider, so that customers and Go Agadir Travel can receive booking confirmations and request details.

Data Protection

We take reasonable steps to protect booking information from unauthorized access, misuse, loss, or disclosure. We only keep information for as long as needed to manage bookings, customer service, legal obligations, and business records.
