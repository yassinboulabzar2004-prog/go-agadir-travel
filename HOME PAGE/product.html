Full description

After pickup from your hotel at 9 AM or 4 PM, this relaxed Agadir activity begins with a scenic journey to the dunes. Along the way, enjoy the beautiful and varied landscapes of the region. You will pass through Aourir, Tamraght, Taghazout, Imi Ouaddar, and Tamri before reaching the sand dunes. After another scenic viewpoint, you can...

enjoy a photo stop, discover the canyon, drink Moroccan tea, try sandboarding in Agadir on the dunes, and watch the sunset before returning to your hotel.
