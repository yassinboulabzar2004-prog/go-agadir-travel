Booking Requests

Submitting a booking form is a request for availability. It does not create a final confirmed booking until Go Agadir Travel confirms the details with you.

Confirmation by Email or WhatsApp

We may confirm booking details by email, WhatsApp, or both. Please make sure your contact details are accurate so we can reach you quickly.

Prices and Availability

Prices, schedules, pickup options, routes, and availability can change due to season, weather, supplier availability, group size, or operational requirements.

Cancellation Policy

Cancellation terms may vary by activity. When possible, we offer flexible cancellation, but some private tours, last-minute bookings, or partner services may have special conditions.

No Payment Today Unless Stated

Most booking requests do not require payment on the website unless clearly stated. Payment details, if needed, will be confirmed before the activity.

Customer Responsibility

Customers are responsible for providing accurate contact information, being ready at the confirmed pickup time, following guide instructions, and choosing activities suitable for their health, age, and comfort level.
